What we do

We design, fabricate and test high-performance formula-styled cars and go-karts for competitions such as SUPRA SAE India, Formula Bharat, and the Morphine Motorsport Series.

Partnering with Team Velocity means supporting student-led engineering work that is tested under real competition pressure—and carrying your brand alongside that journey.
